Choose a wallet whose recovery and verification process you understand before you receive funds. Only download from the project-controlled release pages, and preserve a verified backup before changing devices or software.

## Wallet options

- [Desktop wallet](../wallets/desktop.mdx) is for people who want a full desktop application.
- [Core CLI](../wallets/core-cli.mdx) is for operators and advanced users who are comfortable with command-line tools.
- [Web Wallet](../wallets/web.mdx) provides browser-based access with local-browser and device risks.
- [Android wallet](../wallets/android.mdx) is the mobile option for supported Android devices.
- [Next Wallet](../wallets/next-wallet.mdx) is experimental. Do not use it as a production replacement.

Each guide covers the option's current status, release verification, and relevant safety boundaries. The [wallet overview](../wallets/index.mdx) also compares the choices in one place.

## Before receiving CCX

1. Confirm you are on the intended official release page.
2. Verify the available integrity information for the file you selected.
3. Complete the wallet's backup process and keep the backup private and offline.
4. Make a small, reversible test of the address and workflow before relying on it.

Never share recovery material, wallet passwords, or private keys with a person or website claiming to provide support.
