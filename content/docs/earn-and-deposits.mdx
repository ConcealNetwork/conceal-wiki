Deposit terms, displayed rates, service availability, and withdrawal conditions can change. This page does not quote a standing rate or promise a return. Read the current official service terms and displayed rate on the day you act, then record the date and the exact option you selected for your own records.

## Check the lockup before depositing

1. Identify the amount, term, start condition, and when funds can next be withdrawn.
2. Read the current rate display and terms as a dated offer, not a permanent property of CCX.
3. Confirm whether an early exit is possible, what it changes, and how a completed term is handled.
4. Use a wallet you control and verify the destination and amount with a small test where the service permits it.
5. Keep a private record of the transaction, selected term, and current terms without placing recovery material in that record.

## Risk boundary

Deposits can involve software, counterparty, lockup, network, and market risk. Do not treat a displayed rate as a guarantee, and do not make a decision based on an old article, an unverified social post, or another person's outcome.

Check current network information separately through the official explorer. It reports live state and may change after this page's verification date.
