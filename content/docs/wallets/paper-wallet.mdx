---
title: Paper Wallet
description: Use offline wallet records only with a deliberate physical-security and recovery plan.
---

A paper wallet is an offline record workflow, not a guarantee of safety. Its risks are physical: loss, copying, water or fire damage, untrusted printers, cameras, and transcription mistakes can all prevent recovery or expose funds.

## Before making an offline record

1. Open the official paper wallet at [conceal.network/paperwallet](https://conceal.network/paperwallet/) and check the URL. The [source repository](https://github.com/ConcealNetwork/conceal-paperwallet) is the project record.
2. Use a device and environment you trust. Do not use a public computer or an unknown website.
3. Store the resulting record privately, offline, and in a form you can retrieve after a device failure.
4. Make a second protected copy only if you have a plan to secure both copies independently.
5. Test your recovery process with a small amount before relying on the workflow.

Do not photograph, email, or upload the record. Treat anyone asking for it as untrusted, including someone claiming to provide support.
