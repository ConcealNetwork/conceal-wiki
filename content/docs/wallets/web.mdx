The Web Wallet is browser-based. The verified migration snapshot is **Web Wallet 2.1.4**. Browser use can be convenient, but it ties your wallet workflow to the security and persistence of the browser profile and device you use.

## Browser risks to plan for

- Clearing browser data, changing profiles, replacing a device, or using a compromised extension can interrupt access to locally held wallet data.
- Shared computers, untrusted extensions, and phishing pages increase the risk of exposing sensitive information.
- A browser session is not a recovery plan. Complete and protect your wallet recovery process before relying on browser storage.

## Safer routine

Open the official project release page yourself, check the URL before interacting, and verify any published integrity information for downloadable assets. Keep your browser, operating system, and extensions reviewed and updated. Before making a significant transfer, confirm the intended address with a small test transfer.

Do not send recovery material to a website, support contact, or person who claims they can restore the wallet for you.
