Status: Historical

Last verified: 2026-09-05

The legacy roadmap records Conceal Live in **Q2 2021** as a peer-to-peer, end-to-end encrypted platform for video calls, audio calls, and messages. The dedicated legacy page was later reduced to a discontinuation notice and was last modified on 2024-04-29.

Conceal Live is **discontinued**. This summary exists only to identify the name in material from that period; it is not an availability statement, endorsement, or operational guide. No current setup or connection procedure is preserved here.
