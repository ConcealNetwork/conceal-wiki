Status: Historical

Last verified: 2026-09-05

The legacy roadmap combines planned work and claimed milestones from **April 2018 through Q3 2025**. It records the mainnet launch period, later wallet and network releases, and time-bound product plans. Dates and claims on that page describe their original publication context; they are not a current delivery commitment, supported-product list, software-release schedule, or availability statement.

The legacy media page is a directory of articles, interviews, videos, and mining material from its period. Its deprecated monthly-update section spans **2019 through July 2022**, and the page was last modified on 2023-03-10. This archive does not validate, update, or endorse those external publications.

For current release information, use [Releases & Verification](../releases-and-verification.mdx) and the linked official repositories.

These short historical descriptions are substantially adapted from the cited legacy pages under the GNU Free Documentation License 1.3. They intentionally omit the original promotional claims and operational links.
