description: Availability notice for the retired Conceal Pay service.

Conceal Pay is unavailable. Do not use old payment setup instructions or send funds to an address from them. Conceal does not provide a current merchant, account, or transaction service through Conceal Pay.

For current network and wallet information, use [Network & CCX](../network-and-ccx.mdx) and [Wallets](../wallets/index.mdx).
